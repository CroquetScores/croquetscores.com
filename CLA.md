## 6. Signing

You sign by writing this as a comment on your first pull request:

> I have read the Contributor License Agreement in CLA.md and I agree to it.

A bot records it: your GitHub account number and name, the date, and the pull request, in a file on the `cla-signatures` branch of the repository. That file, and the comment, are the record of your signature. It covers every contribution you make from then on, until you tell the project owner in a GitHub issue that you withdraw from it for contributions made after that. It does not take back what you have already contributed.

If you sign for an organisation, say so in the comment, and that you are authorised to.
