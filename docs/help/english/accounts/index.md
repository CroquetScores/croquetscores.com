# Help for your account

You do not need an account to read a tournament. You need one to run a tournament, and to write on the site. These guides say how to have one, from registering to forgetting it.

- [Registering and confirming your email address](registering.md): the two steps that make an account, and what to do when the email does not come.
- [Signing in](signing-in.md): with a password, with Google or with Facebook, and what to do when you cannot.
- [Passwords](passwords.md): what a password has to be, advice on choosing one, and how the site checks it.
- [Looking after your account](looking-after-your-account.md): your name, your email address, and having the account forgotten.

Each guide stands on its own, so you can read the one you need.
