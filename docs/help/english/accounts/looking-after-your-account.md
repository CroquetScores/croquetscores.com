# Looking after your account

Choose **Profile** in the **Account** menu. It shows your **Name:** and **Email address:**, and leads to the pages that change them.

## Your name

**Change Name** asks for a new **Name**. Your name is shown to your fellow tournament managers.

## Your email address

**Change Email Address** asks for the **New email address** and your **Current password**, and sends a link to the new address. Following it opens a page where **Move my account** moves the account. The password is asked for since an account that is moved is an account that is taken, and being signed in on its own is not enough. An account made with Google, which has no password, cannot be moved this way.

## Your password

**Change Password** and, for an account with none, **Set Password**: see [Passwords](passwords.md).

## Having your account forgotten

**Forget Account** is last on the profile, apart from the rest because it cannot be undone. It sends a link to your email address, and following that link asks you once more before it forgets the account. Your name and email address are then replaced, for good, by "Forgotten Account" and a number. [The privacy page](../privacy.md) says what is kept and what is not.

The last administrator of the site cannot forget their account, since nobody would be left to give the role to anyone else. Give the role to somebody first.
