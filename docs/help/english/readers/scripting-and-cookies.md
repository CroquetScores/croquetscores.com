# Scripting and cookies

## Reading needs neither

The pages that show a tournament, its scores, its players and its rankings work with scripting switched off. So do choosing the theme and the language.

## Cookies

The site sets a cookie to keep you signed in, one for the theme you chose, one for the language you chose, and one for what you have said about notices of what is new. They are there so that the site does what you asked, and are not used to follow you. If you never sign in or choose, there is little for them to hold. [The privacy page](../privacy.md) has the whole of what the site keeps.

A browser that refuses cookies can still read a tournament. It will forget your choices at each visit, and it cannot stay signed in.

## Registering needs scripting

Registering is the one place scripting is needed. The site asks Google's reCAPTCHA to tell a person from a program, and that runs as a script in your browser. If scripting is off when you open the register page, you are sent to a page that says so, before you have filled anything in. Switch scripting on for this site, then go **Back to registering**.

Asking for a way back into an account you cannot sign in to does not need scripting.
