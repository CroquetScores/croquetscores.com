# Finding and following a tournament

None of this needs an account, and all of it works with scripting switched off, except opening a month of a year, which needs scripting (without it a month is a link instead).

## Finding a tournament

The home page lists recent results and upcoming tournaments, and leads to **All tournaments** in the year. A year's page lists its twelve months, closed. Press a month to open its tournaments beneath it, as many as you like at once, or press **Show all** to open every month, and then **Hide all** to close them.

The magnifying glass in the header searches for tournaments, competitions and players. Each list in the results is a table with the days, the sport, the flag of the country and the name.

## Narrowing and sorting a list

The **Country**, **Region** and **Sport** lists, and the **From** and **To** days, narrow a list of tournaments, and **Filter** applies them. Give both days or neither; a tournament is in the range by the day it starts. A range of days is listed a hundred to a page, with **Previous** and **Next**. You can also press a flag to narrow the list to its country.

Press a heading to sort a list by it, and again to turn it round. The sort of each list, and the country, region and sport you chose, are remembered on your phone or computer, so a list begins as you left it. **Back to the usual order** forgets a sorting.

## Reading a competition

A competition offers its **Grid**, **Summary** and **Order of Play** views, and the view you chose is remembered. The order of play can be sorted by **Game** or **Date**. **Print** prints the competition and its results, without the site's bars; it needs scripting.

## Notes and commentaries

A tournament's **Notes** page, headed **Manager's Notes**, lists what its managers have written. A red asterisk marks a note or commentary you have not read, or that has changed since you did. Which you have read is remembered in your browser, so on another device they are marked again.

## What's new

When the help has something new to read, the site says so. Choose **View what's new**, **Remind me next time** or **Never show me this alert**.

## Something wrong

**Report a bug**, at the foot of every page beside **Privacy** and **Terms of use**, tells the people who run the site. To have what the site keeps about you deleted, see the [data deletion policy](../data-deletion-policy.md).

## On a tablet

Held upright, a tablet has the site's links and your account behind a button, as a phone does. Held sideways, they are in the open.
