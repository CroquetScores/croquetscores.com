# Help for readers

You do not need an account to read a tournament's results, and these guides are for whoever is doing that, in a browser on a phone, a tablet or a computer. They say how to have the site the way you like to read it, and what it needs of your browser.

- [Choosing the theme and the language](theme-and-language.md): light or dark, and the language the site speaks to you in.
- [Scripting and cookies](scripting-and-cookies.md): what the site keeps in your browser, and the one thing that needs scripting switched on.
