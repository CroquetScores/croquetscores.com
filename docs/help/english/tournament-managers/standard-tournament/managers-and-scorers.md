# Managers and scorers

A tournament has managers, who can do everything that can be done to it, and may have scorers, who enter its scores and nothing else. Whoever created the tournament is its first manager.

## Managers

The **Managers** tab lists who runs the tournament, in the order they were made, with your own name marked as yours. It is where a tournament is shared, or handed on.

![The Managers tab](images/managers-tab.png)

Somebody is made a manager by their email address. Type it into the field above the list and press **Add Manager**. The address has to belong to an account that is registered and confirmed; an address with none is refused beneath the field, and the site says no more about it than that. A scorer made a manager stops being a scorer.

![An address that cannot be made a manager](images/managers-tab-refused.png)

**Remove** takes a manager off. Nothing is kept of a removed manager, so Remove asks first; they are made a manager again by their address if they are wanted back. A tournament always has a manager, so the last one is not offered Remove.

![The question asked before removing a manager](images/remove-manager-asks.png)

You can take yourself off. The question then says what that means: you lose your own way back to the tournament, and nobody on the page can undo it for you. Another manager can make you one again.

## Scorers

The **Scorers** tab is the Managers tab for scorers, and works the same way: an email address and **Add Scorer** above the list, and **Remove** on each.

![The Scorers tab](images/scorers-tab.png)

A scorer sees the tournament in their own list in the admin, with **Scores** in place of Manage, and is offered its competitions alone: no players, notes, managers, scorers or rankings. On a competition's page they see the Scores tab alone, where they can enter, correct, archive and restore scores as a manager can. Who plays in a competition is the managers' to say.

![A scorer's view of a tournament's page](images/scorer-tournament-page.png)

A manager is not made a scorer, since a manager can already do everything a scorer can. A tournament needs no scorer, so every scorer can be removed, the last among them.
