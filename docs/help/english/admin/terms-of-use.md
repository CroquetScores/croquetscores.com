# Terms of use for managers, scorers and commentators

These are the terms on which you write on this site: as a tournament manager entering a tournament, as a scorer entering results, or as a commentator writing a commentary or a comment on one. You are asked to agree to them when you register and each time you start a commentary. They come on top of the [terms of use](/terms-of-use) every reader agrees to.

## You are responsible for what you write

What you write is published under your name, and it is yours. The views in it are yours, and not necessarily those of the people who run the site.

## Results

Enter results as they were played, and correct them when they turn out to be wrong. Players, their clubs and the world rankings rely on them.

## Commentary

A commentary is for telling readers what is happening at the tournament. It is not a place for good wishes or for conversation.

## What is not allowed

Whatever you write, do not write anything that:

- may stir up violence or hatred, or humiliates anybody for their race, religion, ethnicity, gender, age, disability or sexual orientation;
- attacks a person rather than what they did: play the ball, not the player;
- breaks the law, such as copying what is not yours to copy, or saying something untrue that harms somebody's reputation;
- advertises, sells or solicits, or is spam;
- links to unsuitable material, or links more than it needs to;
- pretends to be somebody else, or gives anybody's phone number, postal address or email address;
- swears, or is written in capitals.

## What the site may do

The people who run the site may change or remove anything written on it that breaks these terms, and may close an account that keeps breaking them.

## Reporting what was written

If you see something on the site that breaks these terms, report it by email, to the address at the foot of this page. Give its web address, quote what was written, and say why it should be removed. Every report is read, but not everything reported is removed.

## Changes

These terms may change. The terms on this page are the ones that apply, and you are asked to agree to them again when you next start a commentary.
