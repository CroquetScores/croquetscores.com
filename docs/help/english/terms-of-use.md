# Terms of use

These are the terms on which you read this site. They are short, because reading asks little of you.

## What the site is

The site shows croquet tournaments: their players, their games and their results, and the commentaries written about them as they are played. It is run by volunteers and paid for by donations.

## What you can rely on

Results are entered by the people who run each tournament, and by the scorers they trust, usually as the games are played. A result can be wrong, late, or corrected afterwards. The tournament's own officials, and not this site, have the final word on a result.

Commentaries and comments are their writers' own. The views in them are not necessarily those of the people who run the site.

## Using what you read

You are welcome to read what the site shows, link to it and share it. Do not copy the site wholesale, or ask it for pages faster than a person reading would: it is run on donations, and that cost is everybody's.

## Writing on the site

If you manage a tournament, enter scores or write commentary, you also agree to the [terms of use for managers, scorers and commentators](/admin/terms-of-use).

## Changes

These terms may change. The terms on this page are the ones that apply.
