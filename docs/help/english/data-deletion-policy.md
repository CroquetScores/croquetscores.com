# Data deletion policy

How to have what this site keeps about you deleted. It applies however you sign in: with Facebook, with Google, or with an email address and a password.

## How to have it deleted

1. Sign in to the account you want deleted, the same way you always do.
2. Open [Forget Account](/account/forget-account), which is also in the account menu and on your profile.
3. Press **Email me the link**. To be sure it is you, the site emails a link to your account's own address. Nothing is deleted until you follow it.
4. Follow the link in the email. It works once, for 15 minutes, and only while you are signed in to the account. It shows what forgetting means.
5. Press **Forget my account**.

You are signed out everywhere you were signed in, and the account can never be signed in to again.

## What is deleted

Your name and your email address are replaced, for good, wherever the site keeps them, by "Forgotten Account" and a number, and an address at example.com that belongs to nobody. Nobody can read them again, the people who run the site included. The same is done to the Google or Facebook account linked to yours, so that nothing ties the account to you. Your email address can then be registered again, as a new account.

## What is kept

Your account's identifier is kept. It is a random number that was made for the account, and it says nothing about who you are. What you published stays with it, under the name "Forgotten Account" and that number: the tournaments you created, the scores you entered, and the commentary and comments you wrote. It is the record of the games, and the people who played in them have a claim to it. It no longer says who you were.

A copy of the site made before you were forgotten, such as a backup, still has what it had then, for as long as the copy is kept.

## Things to know

- **Removing the app from your Facebook settings does not delete anything here.** It only stops Facebook sharing more with the site. To have your data deleted, follow the steps above.
- **It cannot be undone.** That is why it is asked twice.
- **The only administrator cannot be forgotten.** If you are this site's only administrator, give the Administrator role to somebody else first.
- **If you cannot sign in,** use the [Forgot Password](/account/forgot-password) page to set a new password, then sign in and follow the steps above. If you signed in with Facebook or Google, sign in that way instead.

What the site keeps, and why, is in the [privacy statement](/privacy).

## Asking

Anything not said here, or any doubt about whether your data has been deleted, is for the people who run the site.
