# Entering players in a competition

A competition's page is opened from its name on the Competitions tab, or from the dark green bar. It is headed by the competition's name, with two tabs beneath: **Players** and **Scores**. It opens on Players, since the players are entered before anything is played.

A player in a competition is one of the tournament's players, so they are entered by choosing them rather than by typing them. Everything about them but their place in the competition, and what they carry forward into it, is changed on the tournament's Players tab.

![A block's Players tab, with four players entered](images/competition-players-tab.png)

## Adding a player

Above the list, a drop-down offers every player of the tournament who is not already in the competition, in the tournament's order. Choose one and press **Add Player**. The player is put in the tournament's order among those already entered, the drop-down no longer offers them, and the cursor goes back to the drop-down so that the next can be chosen from the keyboard. Where every player of the tournament is in the competition, the drop-down is not shown and the tab says so.

Archived players of the tournament are not offered.

## Byes in a knockout

A knockout is played only once every place in its draw is filled, and the places its players leave over are filled with byes. So in a knockout the drop-down offers **Bye** first, before the players, and goes on offering it however many byes are added, since a draw can need several.

![A knockout of four places with three players and a bye](images/knockout-players.png)

A bye is placed in the draw as a player is: it can be moved up and down and removed, but it cannot withdraw, since it plays nothing. A player entered in a knockout whose every place is filled is refused, and the tab says so beneath the drop-down.

The order of a knockout's players is its draw. The first two places meet in the first match, the next two in the next, and so on. Put the players in the order the draw is to be played in before any score is entered, since once the knockout has a game in use its players are fixed: nobody can be added, removed or moved. A player can still withdraw.

![A knockout once a game has been entered: nobody can be added, moved or removed](images/knockout-players-fixed.png)

## Order

The players are listed in the competition's order, which is the order a knockout is drawn in and a block is listed in on its public page. **Up** and **Down** move a player one place.

## Carry forward

**Carry Forward** says what a player brings into a competition from play it did not score: the results of an earlier stage, or of games played before the competition was scored here. It is offered in a block and a Swiss, which have standings for it to count in, and not in a knockout.

![The Carry Forward dialog](images/carry-forward-dialog.png)

The dialog asks for six counts: matches won and lost, games won and lost, and hoops for and against. Every count is optional. A count left empty carries nothing forward, which is not the same as carrying forward 0, so that whoever changes it later sees it as it was left. Press **Save** to keep all six.

## Removing a player

**Remove** takes out a player entered by mistake. They stay one of the tournament's players, and are offered again in the drop-down, but nothing is kept of their place in the competition, so Remove asks first.

![The question asked before removing a player](images/remove-player-asks.png)

A player who has any game in use in the competition cannot be removed, since the games would then be against somebody who is no longer there, and is not offered Remove. A player whose only games are archived can be, and their archived scores go with them.

## Withdrawing a player

**Withdraw** is for a player who will play no more of the competition. They stay in it, with the games they have played, and are listed under **Withdrawn players** after those still playing. In a knockout, their opponent in a match not yet decided goes through.

Withdraw asks first, since it changes the outcome of the competition: the games the player has not played are decided without them.

![The question asked before withdrawing a player](images/withdraw-player-asks.png)

A withdrawn player offers only **Unwithdraw**, which puts them back among those playing, after those who stayed, without asking.

![A block with a withdrawn player](images/competition-players-withdrawn.png)
