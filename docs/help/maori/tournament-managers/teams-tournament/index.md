# Te whakahaere i tētahi whakataetae ā-kapa

He whakataetae ā-kapa tērā e tūtaki ai ngā taha, kaua ngā kaitākaro ka whakaurua takitahi: he raupapa Test i waenga i ngā whenua e rua, he interstate shield, he whakataetae toa kapa o te ao. Ka whakaritea ōna kapa me ō rātou kaitākaro, ka tāpiria ngā Test i waenga i ngā kapa, ā, ka puta ngā tākaro o ia Test i te wā e tākarohia ana. Ka arahi tēnei aratohu i te kaiwhakahaere mai i te takiuru ki tētahi whakataetae e wātea ana ōna hua ki te marea.

E tū motuhake ana. Kāore he mea i roto e whakawhirinaki ana ki te aratohu mō tētahi whakataetae paerewa, he mea tuhi motuhake tērā.

## I mua i te tīmata

Me whai pūkete koe. Pāwhiria [konei](/account/register) hei rēhita i tētahi pūkete.

Ko ngā mahi katoa a te kaiwhakahaere ka mahia i te wāhanga Whakahaere o te pae tukutuku. Ko ngā whārangi tūmatanui, e whai ai ngā kaitākaro me ngā kaimātaki i te whakataetae, he mea tā mai i ngā mea e puritia ana e te Whakahaere, nō reira kāore he mea e whakaputaina: ka puta tonu ki ngā whārangi tūmatanui tētahi hua ka whakaurua.

## Ngā kupu

Ko te **kapa** he taha, pēnei i a Ahitereiria, he ingoa tōna, he ingoa poto pēnei i te "AUS", me tētahi haki. He **kaitākaro** ake tā te kapa, e tākaro ana mōna, ā, mō tētahi atu kore. Ko te **Test** he whakataetae i waenga i ngā kapa e rua, e kīia ana mā ō rāua ingoa poto, pēnei i te "AUS v ENG", he mea hanga mai i te maha o ngā **tukinga** i waenga i tētahi kaitākaro o ia kapa, ko ia tukinga te pai rawa o te maha o ngā **tākaro**. Ka taea te whakatakoto i ngā Test ki ngā **rōpū**, pēnei i te "Block A", ina tākaro te tini o ngā kapa i waenga i a rātou anō.

## Ngā whārangi i te raupapa

1. [Te waihanga i tētahi whakataetae](creating-a-tournament.md): tōna ingoa, ōna rā, tōna hākinakina, tōna hōputu, me te wāhi e tākarohia ai.
2. [Ngā kapa](teams.md): te tāpiri i ngā kapa, me ō rātou ingoa poto me ō rātou haki.
3. [Ngā kaitākaro o te kapa](team-players.md): te whakauru i ngā kaitākaro o ia kapa ki te whārangi ake o te kapa.
4. [Ngā Test](tests.md): te tāpiri i ngā Test i waenga i ngā kapa, ō rātou rā, te maha o ngā tukinga a ia mea, me ō rātou rōpū.
5. [Ngā hua](scores.md): te whakauru i ngā tākaro o tētahi Test, te whakatika i a rātou, me te whakatārewa i ērā i whakaurua hētia.
6. [Ngā tuhipoka](notes.md): te whakamōhio atu ki ngā kaitākaro i ngā mea e hiahiatia ana e rātou i te haere o te whakataetae.
7. [Ngā kaiwhakahaere me ngā kaikaute](managers-and-scorers.md): te tohatoha i te whakahaere o te whakataetae, me te tuku i tētahi kia whakauru hua ahakoa kāore e taea e ia te whakarerekē i tētahi atu mea.
8. [Te whakarerekē, te pūranga me te whakahoki i tētahi whakataetae](changing-and-archiving.md): te whakatika i ngā taipitopito o te whakataetae, me te whakatārewa i tētahi whakataetae.
9. [Tā te marea e kite ai](what-the-public-sees.md): ngā whārangi e whāia ana e ō kaitākaro, me te āhua e puta ai ki runga i a rātou ngā mea e whakaurua ana e koe.

## Te whakatakotoranga o te Whakahaere

Ko te kuaha o mua o te Whakahaere ka rārangi i ngā whakataetae e whai wāhi ana koe, kotahi te tau i te wā kotahi.

![Te rārangi whakataetae i te Whakahaere](images/admin-list.png)

Ina pēhia te ingoa o tētahi whakataetae, **Whakahaere** rānei, ka tuwhera tōna whārangi. Ko ngā mea katoa hei whakahaere kei roto i ngā ripa e rima i raro i tōna ingoa: **Ngā kapa**, **Ngā Test**, **Ngā tuhipoka**, **Ngā kaiwhakahaere** me **Ngā kaikaute**. Ka tuwhera te whārangi ki Ngā kapa, nā te mea ko ngā kapa te mea tuatahi ka whakaritea.

![Te whārangi o tētahi whakataetae, i tōna ripa Ngā Test](images/tournament-page.png)

Ko te pae kākāriki pōuri i raro i te pane hei neke i waenga i ngā whārangi. I te whārangi o te whakataetae me te whārangi o tētahi Test ka arahi ki te whakataetae, ko **Whakataetae**, me ia Test mā tōna ingoa. I te whārangi o tētahi kapa ka arahi ki te whakataetae me ia kapa mā tōna ingoa poto, nā te mea e whakauru kaitākaro ana te kaiwhakahaere i te whārangi o tētahi kapa, kapa ki te kapa. Ko te whārangi e noho ana koe ki runga kua tohua ki te mangu.

## Ngā mea e tika ana ki ngā wāhi katoa

**Kāore he mea e mukua rawatia haunga te tuhipoka.** Ko ngā kapa, ngā kaitākaro, ngā Test, ngā hua me te whakataetae ake ka pūrangatia, ehara i te muku: ka whakatārewatia, ka rārangi mā roto, ā, ka rārangitia i raro i tētahi pane mā rātou, me **Whakahoki** hei whakahoki mai i a rātou. Kāore te pūranga, te whakahoki me te piki me te heke e pātai kia whakaūngia, nā te mea ka whakakorea ia tētahi ina pēhia tērā atu. Ko ngā mea kāore e taea te whakakore, pēnei i te muku i tētahi tuhipoka, i te tango rānei i tētahi kaiwhakahaere, ka pātai i te tuatahi.

**Kāore he mea e penapenahia e te pouaka kōrero i runga i te whārangi kia pēhia ra anō tāna paopao.** Ka katia ia e te Whakakore, e te pātuhi Escape rānei, ā, ka waiho ngā mea katoa pēnei i mua. Ko ngā pouaka kōrero e tāpiri ana i ngā mea, pēnei i a Tāpiri kapa me Tāpiri hua, ka noho tuwhera i muri i te tāpiri o ia mea, kia taea ai te tuhi i tētahi rārangi, tētahi i muri i tētahi.

**Ko te mea e whakahēngia ka kīia i te taha o te āpure e pā ana ki a ia.** Kāore he mea i whakahēngia e penapenahia, ā, ka noho tonu ngā mea i tuhia e koe, kia taea ai te whakatika, kaua te tuhi anō.

**Ka mahi te Whakahaere i runga i te waea pūkoro.** He rahi tonu ia paopao hei pēhi mā te kōmaru, ā, kāore he mea e whānui ake ana i te mata.

E whai ake nei: [Te waihanga i tētahi whakataetae](creating-a-tournament.md).
