# Tā te marea e kite ai

Kāore he mea e whakaputaina: he mea tā ngā whārangi tūmatanui mai i ngā mea e puritia ana e te Whakahaere, nō reira ka puta tonu tētahi hua ka whakaurua ki runga. Kāore rātou e hiahia pūkete, tuhinga hōtaka rānei, ā, kua whakatakotoria mō te waea pūkoro.

## Te whārangi o te whakataetae

Kei tōna tau, tōna hākinakina me tōna ingoa te whārangi o te whakataetae, pēnei i te `/2026/ac/trans-tasman-tests`. Ko te ingoa o te whakataetae tōna pane, me tōna taitara iti i raro. Ka rārangi i ia tākaro e whakamahia ana, ko te mea i tāpiritia tata nei i te tuatahi, kia kite ai te kaipānui ka hoki mai i ngā mea i puta mai i tērā wā. Ka kī ia tākaro i te toa, tōna kapa me tōna hua, kātahi ko te hinga me tāna, me te Test i tākarohia ai, me te roa mai i te tāpiritanga.

![Te whārangi tūmatanui o te whakataetae](../../../english/tournament-managers/teams-tournament/images/public-tournament.png)

Ka tuku te pae kākāriki pōuri i ngā wāhanga o te whakataetae: **Tata nei**, koia tēnei whārangi, **Whakarāpopototanga**, **Ngā kaitākaro**, **Ngā tuhipoka** me **Ngā kōrero**, kātahi ko ngā Test. Ka kīia tētahi Test mā ngā ingoa poto o ōna kapa me "ki" i waenga, pēnei i te "AUS ki NZ". Kāore ngā Test o tētahi rōpū e tukuna takitahi: he wāhanga kotahi te rōpū, he mea whakaingoa pēnei i te rōpū, e tū ana ki te wāhi e tū ai tana Test tuatahi. Ka tohua Ngā tuhipoka ki te tohu whetū whero i te wā he tuhipoka kāore anō kia pānuitia e te kaipānui.

## Te whakarāpopototanga

He **Whakarāpopototanga** tō te whakataetae kāore ōna Test i tētahi rōpū: he tēpu kotahi o ia kapa, kua raupapatia, me ngā Test kua toa, kua hinga, kua taurite ai ia, ōna tākaro i toa, i hinga, me ōna hupa mō, ki me te tapeke. Ina ko ngā tukinga o tētahi Test te pai rawa o te nui ake i te kotahi, ka whai ngā tukinga i toa, i hinga i muri i ngā Test. Ko te whakarāpopototanga te wāhi e rapu ai te kaipānui i ia kapa hei katoa.

Kāore he Whakarāpopototanga a te whakataetae he Test tōna kei tētahi rōpū. Ka whakaatu te whārangi o ia rōpū i te tūnga o ngā kapa o taua rōpū kē, nā te mea ko tētahi tēpu kotahi o ngā kapa puta noa i ngā rōpū kāore nei i tākaro tētahi ki tētahi, kāore e raupapa i tētahi mea.

## He rōpū

Ka whakaatu te whārangi o tētahi rōpū i te tūnga o ngā kapa o te rōpū, ka kaute i ngā Test o te rōpū anake, i te tēpu e whakamahia ana e te whakarāpopototanga. I runga i te tēpu, i tētahi rārangi mōna ake, ka tuku i a **Whakarāpopototanga**, koia tēnei whārangi, me ia Test o te rōpū, e arahi ana ki te whārangi o te Test.

![Te whārangi o tētahi rōpū](../../../english/tournament-managers/teams-tournament/images/public-group.png)

## He Test

Ko ngā ingoa katoa o ōna kapa te pane o te whārangi o tētahi Test, pēnei i a "Australia ki New Zealand", ā, ka kī i te pai rawa o te aha o ia tukinga. Ko ngā kapa e rua te pane o tōna tēpu, ko te tuatahi kei te mauī. He rārangi ia tukinga kua tākarohia, i te raupapa i tīmata ai, ko te kaitākaro o te kapa tuatahi kei te mauī, ko te kaitākaro o te kapa tuarua kei te matau, me ngā tākaro o te tukinga i waenga i a rāua, ko te hua o te taha tuatahi i te tuatahi, kātahi ko te taha tuarua, kua tohua te toa. I raro i ngā tukinga ko ngā tākaro me ngā hupa a ia kapa, ā, ina ko ia tukinga te pai rawa o te nui ake i te kotahi, ōna tukinga i toa, i tōna ake taha.

![Te whārangi o tētahi Test](../../../english/tournament-managers/teams-tournament/images/public-test.png)

E rua ngā huarahi e whakaaturia ai tētahi Test, ka tukuna i te rārangi o tōna ingoa: **Tūtohi**, ko tēnei tēpu, me **Raupapa tākaro**, e rārangi ana i ia tākaro o te Test, ko te tawhito i te tuatahi. Ka tuku tētahi Test o tētahi rōpū i te Whakarāpopototanga me ngā Test o tōna rōpū i tētahi rārangi i runga i tōna pane, kua tohua tēnei Test.

## Ngā kaitākaro

Ka rārangi te whārangi Ngā kaitākaro i ngā kaitākaro kapa ki te kapa: ia kapa i raro i tētahi pane o tōna ingoa, i te raupapa o ngā kapa o te whakataetae, me ōna kaitākaro i raro i te raupapa pūrei.

![Te whārangi tūmatanui Ngā kaitākaro o te whakataetae](../../../english/tournament-managers/teams-tournament/images/public-players.png)

## Ngā tuhipoka

Ka rārangi te whārangi Ngā tuhipoka i ngā tuhipoka o te whakataetae, ko te mea i tuhia tata nei i te tuatahi, ia mea e arahi ana ki tōna ake whārangi, e kī ana i te roa mai i tōna tuhinga. Ka tohua te tuhipoka kāore anō kia pānuitia e te kaipānui ki te tohu whetū whero kia tuwheratia ra anō e rātou.

## Ngā wāhitau e neke ana

Ka mau tonu i te whakataetae tōna wāhitau ina whakatikaina tōna ingoa. Ina neke, nā te mea i rerekē tōna tau, tōna hākinakina rānei, ka arahi tōna wāhitau tawhito ki te hou, kia mahi tonu ai te hononga i puritia mai i mua. Kāore ngā whārangi o te whakataetae kua pūrangatia e kitea kia whakahokia ra anō.
