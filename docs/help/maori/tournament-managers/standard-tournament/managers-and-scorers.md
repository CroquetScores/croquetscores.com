# Ngā kaiwhakahaere me ngā kaitākohu

He kaiwhakahaere tā tētahi whakataetae, ka taea e rātou ngā mea katoa e taea ana te mahi ki a ia, ā, tērā pea he kaitākohu tāna, ka whakauru i ōna tākohu, ā, kāore atu. Ko te tangata nāna i waihanga te whakataetae tōna kaiwhakahaere tuatahi.

## Ngā kaiwhakahaere {#managers}

Ka rārangitia e te ripa **Kaiwhakahaere** ko wai kei te whakahaere i te whakataetae, i te raupapa i meinga ai rātou, ā, kua tohua tō ingoa ake hei mea nōu. Ko te wāhi tēnei e tohatohahia ai tētahi whakataetae, e tukuna ai rānei ki tētahi atu.

![Te ripa Kaiwhakahaere](images/managers-tab.png)

Ka meinga tētahi hei kaiwhakahaere mā tōna wāhitau īmēra. Tuhia ki te āpure i runga i te rārangi, ka pēhi i **Tāpiri kaiwhakahaere**. Me whai te wāhitau i tētahi pūkete kua rēhitatia, kua whakaūngia hoki; ka whakahēngia tētahi wāhitau kāore he pūkete i raro i te āpure, ā, kāore te pae e kī atu i tua atu i tērā. Ka kore te kaitākohu kua meinga hei kaiwhakahaere e noho hei kaitākohu.

![He wāhitau kāore e taea te meinga hei kaiwhakahaere](images/managers-tab-refused.png)

Ka tango **Tango** i tētahi kaiwhakahaere. Kāore he mea e penapenahia mō te kaiwhakahaere kua tangohia, nō reira ka pātai a Tango i te tuatahi; ka meinga anō ia hei kaiwhakahaere mā tōna wāhitau mēnā e hiahiatia ana kia hoki mai. He kaiwhakahaere tonu tā tētahi whakataetae, nō reira kāore a Tango e tukuna ki te whakamutunga.

![Te pātai ka pātaihia i mua i te tango i tētahi kaiwhakahaere](images/remove-manager-asks.png)

Ka taea e koe te tango i a koe ake. Ka kī te pātai i te tikanga o tērā: ka ngaro tō ake huarahi hoki ki te whakataetae, ā, kāore tētahi i te whārangi e taea te whakakore mōu. Ka taea e tētahi atu kaiwhakahaere te mea anō koe hei kaiwhakahaere.

## Ngā kaitākohu {#scorers}

Ko te ripa **Kaitākohu** te ripa Kaiwhakahaere mō ngā kaitākohu, ā, he ōrite tana mahi: he wāhitau īmēra me **Tāpiri kaitākohu** i runga i te rārangi, me **Tango** i ia mea.

![Te ripa Kaitākohu](images/scorers-tab.png)

Ka kite te kaitākohu i te whakataetae i tōna ake rārangi i te Kaiwhakahaere, me **Ngā tākohu** hei whakakapi mō Kaiwhakahaere, ā, ka tukuna ki a ia ōna tauwhāinga anake: kāore he kaitākaro, tuhinga, kaiwhakahaere, kaitākohu, raupapa rānei. I te whārangi o tētahi tauwhāinga ka kite ia i te ripa Ngā tākohu anake, ka taea e ia te whakauru, te whakatika, te whakatāpui me te whakahoki i ngā tākohu pēnei i te kaiwhakahaere. Ko ngā kaiwhakahaere anake e kī ai ko wai ka tākaro i tētahi tauwhāinga.

![Te tirohanga a te kaitākohu ki te whārangi o tētahi whakataetae](images/scorer-tournament-page.png)

Kāore te kaiwhakahaere e meinga hei kaitākohu, nā te mea ka taea e te kaiwhakahaere ngā mea katoa e taea ana e te kaitākohu. Kāore tētahi whakataetae e hiahia kaitākohu, nō reira ka taea te tango i ngā kaitākohu katoa, tae atu ki te whakamutunga o rātou.
