# Tā te marea e kite ai

Kāore he mea e whakaputaina: he mea tā ngā whārangi tūmatanui mai i ngā mea e puritia ana e te Whakahaere, nō reira ka puta tonu tētahi hua ka whakaurua ki runga. Kāore rātou e hiahia pūkete, tuhinga hōtaka rānei, ā, kua whakatakotoria mō te waea pūkoro.

## Te whārangi o te whakataetae

Kei tōna tau, tōna hākinakina me tōna ingoa te whārangi o te whakataetae, pēnei i te `/2026/ac/spring-open`. Ko te ingoa o te whakataetae tōna pane, me tōna taitara iti i raro. Ka rārangi i ia tākaro e whakamahia ana, ko te mea i tāpiritia tata nei i te tuatahi, kia kite ai te kaipānui ka hoki mai i ngā mea i puta mai i tērā wā. Ka kī ia tākaro i te toa, te mea e tohu ana ia, tōna haki me ōna tohu, kātahi ko te hinga me ōna, me te tauwhāinga i tākarohia ai, me te roa mai i te tāpiritanga.

![Te whārangi tūmatanui o te whakataetae](../../../english/tournament-managers/standard-tournament/images/public-tournament.png)

Ka tuku te pae kākāriki pōuri i ngā wāhanga o te whakataetae: **Tata nei**, koia tēnei whārangi, **Ngā kaitākaro**, **Ngā tuhipoka** me **Ngā kōrero**, kātahi ko ia tauwhāinga e whakamahia ana, i te raupapa o te ripa Ngā tauwhāinga. Ka tohua Ngā tuhipoka ki te tohu whetū whero i te wā he tuhipoka kāore anō kia pānuitia e te kaipānui.

## Te poraka

Ka whakaatu te whārangi o tētahi poraka i ōna hua hei tūtohi: he haupae, he tīwae mō ia kaitākaro i te raupapa o te poraka, me ngā tākaro i waenga i ngā kaitākaro e rua i te wāhi e tūtaki ai te haupae me te tīwae, ia mea kua tohua hei toa, hei hinga, hei taurite rānei mō te kaitākaro o te haupae. I muri i ngā tīwae o ngā kaitākaro ko ngā tapeke o ia kaitākaro. Ina kotahi te tākaro o ia tukinga, he toa me ngā tapeke hupa ērā; ina ko te tukinga te pai rawa o te nui ake i te kotahi, he tukinga, he tapeke tākaro, he tākaro me ngā tapeke hupa.

![Te tūtohi o tētahi poraka](../../../english/tournament-managers/standard-tournament/images/public-block-grid.png)

E toru ngā huarahi e whakaaturia ai te poraka, ka tukuna i te rārangi o tōna ingoa: **Tūtohi**, **Whakarāpopototanga** me **Raupapa tākaro**. Ko te whakarāpopototanga te mea mō te waea pūkoro: ngā kaitākaro kua raupapatia, me ō rātou toa, hinga me ngā tapeke hupa. Ka rārangi te raupapa tākaro i ia tākaro, ko te tawhito i te tuatahi. Ka mau tonu i te kaipānui ka kōwhiri i tētahi tirohanga taua tirohanga mō ia tauwhāinga kia kōwhiria ra anō e ia tētahi atu.

![Te whakarāpopototanga o tētahi poraka](../../../english/tournament-managers/standard-tournament/images/public-block-summary.png)

Ka kī te haupae o te kaitākaro kua unuhia i pērā. Ka whakaaturia te hua kua unuhia me te rārangi mā roto, ā, kāore e kaute. Kāore te hua kua pūrangatia e whakaaturia.

## Te whakawātea

Ka tā te whārangi o tētahi whakawātea i tōna tūtohi ia rauna, he tīwae mō ia rauna mai i te tuatahi ki te whakamutunga, me ngā taha e rua o ia tukinga tētahi i runga i tētahi, me te pūtau mō ia tākaro o te pai rawa o te tukinga. Ka tū te tukinga o te rauna ā muri ki te taha o ngā tukinga e rua ko ō rāua toa kei roto. I te waea pūkoro ka panuku whakapae te tūtohi, me ngā pere ki te whakamutunga, ki muri hoki.

![Te tūtohi o tētahi whakawātea](../../../english/tournament-managers/standard-tournament/images/public-knockout.png)

## Te Swiss

Ka raupapa te whārangi o tētahi Swiss i ōna kaitākaro ki te tēpu: ā rātou tākaro, ā rātou toa, tō rātou ōrau toa, me ā rātou hua, ia mea ka kīia hei hoa whawhai me te rerenga, pēnei i te "+4" mō te tākaro i toa mā te whā.

## Ngā kaitākaro

Ka rārangi te whārangi Ngā kaitākaro i ia kaitākaro o te whakataetae kāore i pūrangatia, i te raupapa pūrei, me te mea e tohu ana rātou me tō rātou haki.

## Ngā tuhipoka

Ka rārangi te whārangi Ngā tuhipoka i ngā tuhipoka o te whakataetae, ko te mea i tuhia tata nei i te tuatahi, ia mea e arahi ana ki tōna ake whārangi, e kī ana i te roa mai i tōna tuhinga. Ka tohua te tuhipoka kāore anō kia pānuitia e te kaipānui ki te tohu whetū whero kia tuwheratia ra anō e rātou.

![Te whārangi tūmatanui Ngā tuhipoka o te whakataetae](../../../english/tournament-managers/standard-tournament/images/public-notes.png)

## Ngā wāhitau e neke ana

Ka mau tonu i te whakataetae tōna wāhitau ina whakatikaina tōna ingoa. Ina neke, nā te mea i rerekē tōna tau, tōna hākinakina rānei, ka arahi tōna wāhitau tawhito ki te hou, kia mahi tonu ai te hononga i puritia mai i mua. Kāore ngā whārangi o te whakataetae kua pūrangatia e kitea kia whakahokia ra anō.
