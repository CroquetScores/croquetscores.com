# Āwhina mā ngā kaiwhakahaere whakataetae

Ka whakahaerehia tētahi whakataetae i tēnei pae mai i te Whakahaere e ōna kaiwhakahaere: te tangata nāna i waihanga, me te hunga katoa kua meinga e ia hei kaiwhakahaere i muri mai. Ka whakaatu ēnei aratohu i te huarahi, mai i te takiuru ki tētahi whakataetae e wātea ana ōna hua ki te marea.

E rua ngā momo whakataetae, ā, he aratohu mō ia momo. E tū motuhake ana ia aratohu, nō reira ka taea e koe te pānui i tērā mō te whakataetae e whakahaerehia ana e koe, ā, kāore atu.

- [Te whakahaere i tētahi whakataetae paerewa](standard-tournament/index.md): he whakataetae i waenga i ngā kaitākaro ka whakaurua takitahi, ki ngā poraka, ki ngā whakawātea, ki ngā Swiss. He mutunga wiki a te karapu, he whakataetae tuwhera, he whakataetae toa.
- [Te whakahaere i tētahi whakataetae ā-kapa](teams-tournament/index.md): he whakataetae i waenga i ngā kapa, hei Test i waenga i ngā taha e rua, he mea hanga mai i ngā tukinga i waenga i ō rātou kaitākaro. He raupapa Test, he interstate shield, he whakataetae toa kapa o te ao.

Ko te **Hōputu** o te whakataetae i tōna waihangatanga te mea e whakatau ai ko tēhea momo: Paerewa mō te tuatahi, ā, Ā-kapa, Eire Cup, Interstate Shield, GC World Team Championship rānei mō te tuarua.
