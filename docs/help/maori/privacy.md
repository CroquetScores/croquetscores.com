# Matatapu

Ngā mea e puritia ana e tēnei pae mōu, he aha te take e whakamahia ana, me te āhua e warewaretia ai.

## Ngā mea e puritia ana

- **Tō wāhitau īmēra me tō ingoa**, mēnā ka rēhita koe. Ka puritia ngā mea e rua kua whakamunatia, me tētahi kī e whai wāhi ana ki tō pūkete anake. Ka puritia hoki tō wāhitau īmēra ki tētahi āhua e kitea ai e te pae tō pūkete ina takiuru koe, ā, e kore e taea te whakahoki ki te wāhitau.
- **Tō kupuhipa**, mēnā ka rēhita koe me tētahi, hei hash e kore e taea te whakahoki ki te kupuhipa. E kore e purihia e te pae te kupuhipa ake, ā, e kore e taea e ia te kī atu he aha ia.
- **Ko te mea e takiuru ai koe**, mēnā ka takiuru koe mā Google, mā Facebook rānei: te tohu e hoatu ai e rātou ki te pae mōu, me tō wāhitau īmēra ina tohaina e rātou. Kāore he mea atu o rātou e puritia.
- **Ngā mea e whakauru ai koe**: ngā whakataetae e hanga ai koe, ngā kaute e tāuru ai koe, me ngā kōrero me ngā tākupu e tuhi ai koe. He mea tūmatanui ērā ki te wāhi e whakaatu ai te pae ki te marea.
- **Te wāhitau i tae mai ai tētahi tono**, mō ia whakamātau takiuru i rahua me ia tono mō tētahi kupuhipa hou, me te wā i mahia ai. Ka puritia kia taea ai te whakaroa i te matapae kupuhipa, i te whakakī rawa rānei i tētahi pouaka mēra, ā, mō tētahi atu mea kāore.

## Ngā mea e kore e puritia

E kore te pae e whakamahi i ngā panui, i te aru rānei, ā, e kore ia e tohatoha i tētahi mea mōu ki ngā kaipanui.

## Ngā pihikete

Ka whakatakoto te pae i tētahi pihikete hei pupuri i a koe kua takiuru, tētahi mō te kaupapa i kōwhiria e koe, tētahi mō te reo i kōwhiria e koe, me tētahi mō ngā mea kua kī koe mō ngā whakamōhiotanga o ngā mea hou. E hiahiatia ana hei mahi i ngā mea i tonoa e koe e te pae, ā, e kore e whakamahia hei aru i a koe. Ka whakamahi te rēhitatanga i a Google reCAPTCHA hei wehewehe i te tangata i ngā hōtaka, ā, e whakahaerehia ana e Google i raro i ōna ake here.

## Ko wai e kite i a ia

Ka whakamunatia tō ingoa i te wā okioki, ā, ko ō hoa kaiwhakahaere whakataetae anake e kite i a ia. Ka puritia hoki tō wāhitau īmēra kua whakamunatia, ā, ka pānuitia anake e te pae hei mahi i tāu i tono ai, pērā i te tuku īmēra ki a koe. Ko ngā tāngata e whakahaere ana i te pae, e tiaki ana i te pātengi raraunga e puritia ai.

## Īmēra

Ka tukua e te pae he īmēra hei whakatūturu i tētahi wāhitau īmēra, hei whakarite i tētahi kupuhipa, hei kī atu rānei kua rerekētia, hei kī atu kua nuku tētahi pūkete ki tētahi wāhitau īmēra hou, me te whakawareware i tētahi pūkete, ā, kāore he mea atu ki a koe. Ka haere mā te ratonga e whakamahia ana e te pae hei tuku mēra.

## Te whakawareware

Ka taea e koe te whakawareware i tō pūkete i ngā wā katoa mai i tō kōtaha ina takiuru koe. Ka meinga tō ingoa me tō wāhitau īmēra kia kore e taea te pānui mō ake tonu, ā, e kore e taea e tētahi te whakahoki, tae atu ki a koe. Ka noho tonu ngā mea i whakaputaina e koe, i raro i te ingoa "Account Forgotten" (te ingoa Ingarihi). E kore e taea te whakakore i te warewaretanga, koia i uiuia ai e rua ngā wā, ā, ka taea te rēhita anō i te wāhitau īmēra hei pūkete hou.

## Te pātai

Ko ngā mea katoa kāore i kōrerotia i konei, ko ngā pōauau rānei mō ngā mea e puritia ana mōu, mā ngā tāngata e whakahaere ana i te pae.
