# Āwhina mō tō pūkete

Kāore koe e hiahia ki tētahi pūkete hei pānui i tētahi whakataetae. Me whai pūkete koe hei whakahaere i tētahi whakataetae, hei tuhi hoki ki runga i te pae. Ko ēnei aratohu te kōrero mō te whai pūkete, mai i te rēhita ki te warewaretia.

- [Te rēhita me te whakaū i tō wāhitau īmēra](registering.md): ngā wāhanga e rua hei waihanga pūkete, me te mahi ina kore e tae mai te īmēra.
- [Te takiuru](signing-in.md): mā te kupuhipa, mā Google, mā Facebook rānei, me te mahi ina kore e taea.
- [Ngā kupuhipa](passwords.md): ngā mea e tika ana mō tētahi kupuhipa, ngā tohutohu mō te kōwhiri i tētahi, me tā te pae tirotiro i a ia.
- [Te tiaki i tō pūkete](looking-after-your-account.md): tō ingoa, tō wāhitau īmēra, me te warewaretia o te pūkete.

He tū motuhake ia aratohu, nō reira ka taea e koe te pānui i tērā e hiahiatia ana e koe.
