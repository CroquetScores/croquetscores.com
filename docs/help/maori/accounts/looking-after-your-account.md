# Te tiaki i tō pūkete

Kōwhiria **Kōtaha** i te tahua **Pūkete**. Ka whakaatu i tō **Ingoa:** me tō **Wāhitau īmēra:**, ā, ka arahi ki ngā whārangi hei whakarerekē i aua mea.

## Tō ingoa

Ka pātai **Whakarerekē ingoa** i tētahi **Ingoa** hou. Ka whakaatuhia tō ingoa ki ō hoa kaiwhakahaere whakataetae.

## Tō wāhitau īmēra

Ka pātai **Whakarerekē wāhitau īmēra** i te **Wāhitau īmēra hou** me tō **Kupuhipa o nāianei**, ā, ka tuku i tētahi hononga ki te wāhitau hou. Ka whāia, ka tuwhera tētahi whārangi e nuku ai te pūkete mā **Nekehia taku pūkete**. Ka pātaihia te kupuhipa nā te mea ko te pūkete e nuku ana, he pūkete e tāhaetia ana, ā, kāore i te ranea te takiuru anake. Kāore e taea te nuku pēnei i tētahi pūkete i waihangatia mā Google, kāore nei he kupuhipa.

## Tō kupuhipa

**Whakarerekē kupuhipa** me, mō te pūkete kāore he kupuhipa, **Tautuhi kupuhipa**: tirohia [Ngā kupuhipa](passwords.md).

## Te warewaretia o tō pūkete

Ko **Warewaretia te pūkete** te mea whakamutunga i te kōtaha, he mea wehe, nā te mea kāore e taea te whakakore. Ka tuku i tētahi hononga ki tō wāhitau īmēra, ā, ina whāia taua hononga ka pātai anō i a koe kotahi anō i mua i te warewaretia o te pūkete. Ka kore e taea te pānui tō ingoa me tō wāhitau īmēra. Kei [Te whārangi matatapu](../privacy.md) ngā mea e puritia ana, me ngā mea e kore e puritia.

Kāore e taea e te kaiwhakahaere pae whakamutunga te warewaretia tōna pūkete, nā te mea kāore he tangata e toe hei hoatu i te tūranga ki tētahi atu. Hoatu te tūranga ki tētahi atu tangata i te tuatahi.
