# Āwhina mā ngā kaipānui

Kāore he pūkete e hiahiatia ana hei pānui i ngā hua o tētahi whakataetae, ā, mā te tangata e pērā ana ēnei aratohu, i tētahi pūtirotiro i runga i te waea, i te papa rorohiko, i te rorohiko rānei. E kī ana rātou i te āhua e pai ai ki a koe te pae hei pānui, me ngā mea e hiahiatia ana e tō pūtirotiro.

- [Te kōwhiri i te kaupapa tae me te reo](theme-and-language.md): marama, pōuri rānei, me te reo e kōrero ai te pae ki a koe.
- [Te tuhinga kōkiri me ngā pihikete](scripting-and-cookies.md): ngā mea e puritia ana e te pae ki tō pūtirotiro, me te kotahi anake e hiahiatia ana te tuhinga kōkiri kia whakaaetia.
