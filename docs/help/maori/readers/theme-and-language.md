# Te kōwhiri i te kaupapa tae me te reo

Kei raro i ngā whārangi katoa ēnei kōwhiringa e rua, ā, e maumahara ana te pae ki ngā kōwhiringa e rua, nō reira kotahi anake te wā e kōwhiri ai koe.

## Te kaupapa tae

Ka tāpaetia e **Kaupapa tae** ko **Marama**, ko **Pōuri** me **Pūnaha**. Ko **Pūnaha**, ko te mea kei a koe kia kōwhiri noa koe, ka whai i tō waea, i tō rorohiko rānei: ka pōuri te pae ina pōuri tērā, ka marama ina kāore. Kōwhiria **Marama**, **Pōuri** rānei kia pērā tonu te pae ahakoa te aha o tō pūrere.

## Te reo

Kia kōwhiri noa koe, ka kōrero te pae i te reo tuatahi e kī ana tō pūtirotiro e pānui ana koe, ā, kei te pae. Ki te kore he reo o ēnei kei a ia, ka kōrero Pākehā.

Hei kōwhiri i tētahi atu, kōwhiria i te rārangi **Reo**, ka pēhi i **Whakarerekē**. Ka hoki mai te whārangi i taua reo, pēnei anō hoki ngā whārangi katoa i muri mai, i tēnei haerenga, i te haerenga e whai ake nei hoki. Ka tuwhera te rārangi i te reo e pānui ana koe.

Ko tētahi whārangi o tēnei āwhina kāore anō kia whakapākehātia ki tō reo, ka whakaatuhia i te reo Pākehā, kaua e huna.

## Mehemea ka warewaretia tētahi kōwhiringa

Ka maumahara te pae ki ia kōwhiringa i tētahi pihikete, nō reira ko tētahi pūtirotiro e whakawātea ana i ōna pihikete, e whakaritea ana kia kaua e pupuri, ka warewaretia, ā, ka tono kia kōwhiri anō koe. Ka kī [Te tuhinga kōkiri me ngā pihikete](scripting-and-cookies.md) he aha ngā mea e puritia ana e te pae.

E whai ake nei: [Te tuhinga kōkiri me ngā pihikete](scripting-and-cookies.md).
