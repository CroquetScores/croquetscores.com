# He aha te mea hou

## I tukuna 2026-10-27

Kua tuhia katoatia anōtia a Croquet Scores ki te AI, i te TypeScript. Koinei ngā mea ka kite koe.

### Te kimi i ngā mea

- **Rapua.** He pouaka rapu kei runga i ia whārangi. Patohia he ingoa, ka pēhi i a Rapua hei kimi i ngā whakataetae nui, ngā tauwhāinga me ngā kaitākaro. E toru ngā rārangi tākohu, ā, ka tukuna atu te rārangi kāore he mea i roto. Mēnā neke atu i te rau o tētahi momo, ka kī te whārangi, ā, mā te tāpiri i tētahi kupu ki tāu rapu e iti ai te maha.
- **Whakaitihia ngā whakataetae nui mā te whenua, te rohe me te hākinakina.** I te whārangi kāinga, kōwhiria he whenua kia kite ai i ngā whakataetae nui e tākarohia ana i reira anake. Ina kōwhiri koe i tētahi whenua, ka taea e koe te kōwhiri i tētahi kāwanatanga o taua whenua, me tētahi hākinakina hoki. Kei te wāhitau o te whārangi ō kōwhiringa, nā reira ka taea e koe te tohu i te whārangi, te tuku rānei ki tētahi atu, ā, ka whakakorea e te pātene hoki. **Ko te kōwhiri i tētahi kāwanatanga ka kitea anake ngā whakataetae nui hou.** Kāore ngā mea tawhito i hoatu he kāwanatanga, nā reira ka waihohia atu i te kōwhiringa.

### Te whai i tētahi whakataetae nui

- **Ngā tuhinga mai i ngā kaiwhakahaere.** Ka taea e ngā kaiwhakahaere o tētahi whakataetae nui te tuhi tuhinga inaianei hei kī atu ki ōna kaitākaro i ngā mea hei mōhio mā rātou i a ia e tākarohia ana: te whakatakotoranga, tētahi panoni ki te raupapa tākaro, te wā o te tina. Kei te wāhanga hou o te whakataetae nui, **Ngā tuhinga**, te mea hōu rawa i te tuatahi, me te roa o te wā mai i tuhia ai ia tuhinga.
- **He whetū (\*) hei tohu i ngā mea kāore anō kia pānuitia.** He whetū tō te tuhinga kia tae ra ano ki te wā ka whakatuwheratia e koe, ā, ka puta anō mēnā ka whakarerekētia i muri i tō pānui. Ina kāore i pānuitia tētahi tuhinga, he whetū tō te wāhanga Ngā tuhinga hoki, i ia whārangi o te whakataetae nui, kia kite ai koe he kōrero hou kāore e haere ki te kimi. Ka tohua ngā kōrero i te ara ōrite. Ka maumahara tō pūtirotiro i ngā mea kua pānuitia e koe, nā reira kāore koe e hiahia ki te takiuru. Ka tīmata tētahi atu waea, rorohiko rānei me te kore he mea kua pānuitia.
- **He māmā ake te whai i ngā kōrero.** I te pae tawhito, ka maka i te whārangi ki raro rawa i ia kōrero hou, nā reira ka ngaro te wāhi o te tangata e titiro whakamuri ana ki ngā kōrero o mua. Inaianei ka neke anake te whārangi ki raro ki tētahi kōrero hou mēnā kei raro rawa koe, e pānui ana i te mea hōu rawa. Mēnā kua retireti whakamuri koe ki te pānui i ngā kōrero o mua, ka waiho koe ki te wāhi e noho ana koe.
