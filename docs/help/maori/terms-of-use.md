# Ngā kupu whakamahi

Koinei ngā kupu whakamahi e pānui ai koe i tēnei pae. He poto, nā te mea he iti noa te tono a te pānui ki a koe.

## He aha te pae

Ka whakaatu te pae i ngā whakataetae croquet, i ō rātou kaitākaro, i ā rātou kēmu me ā rātou putanga, me ngā kōrero e tuhia ana mō rātou i a rātou e tākarohia ana. Nā ngā tāngata tūao e whakahaere, ā, nā ngā koha e utu.

## Ngā mea e whakawhirinaki ai koe

Nā ngā tāngata e whakahaere ana i ia whakataetae ngā putanga e whakauru, me ngā kaitākohu e whakapono ana rātou, i te nuinga o te wā i a rātou e tākarohia ana. Tērā pea he hē tētahi putanga, he tōmuri, ka whakatikaina rānei i muri mai. Ko ngā āpiha o te whakataetae ake, ehara i tēnei pae, te kupu whakamutunga mō tētahi putanga.

Nā ō rātou kaituhi ake ngā kōrero me ngā whakaaro. Ehara ngā tirohanga o roto i ērā i te mea ko ngā tirohanga o ngā tāngata e whakahaere ana i te pae.

## Te whakamahi i ngā mea e pānuitia ana e koe

Nau mai ki te pānui i ngā mea e whakaatu ana te pae, ki te hono atu ki a ia, ki te tohatoha hoki. Kaua e tāruatia te pae katoa, kaua hoki e tonoa he whārangi e tere ake ana i te tangata e pānui ana: e whakahaerehia ana i runga i ngā koha, ā, ko taua utu he mea nā te katoa.

## Te tuhi ki te pae

Mēnā ka whakahaere koe i tētahi whakataetae, ka whakauru kaute, ka tuhi kōrero rānei, ka whakaae hoki koe ki [ngā kupu whakamahi mō ngā kaiwhakahaere, ngā kaitākohu me ngā kaituhi kōrero](/admin/terms-of-use).

## Ngā panonitanga

Tērā pea ka panoni ēnei kupu whakamahi. Ko ngā kupu whakamahi i runga i tēnei whārangi ērā e whai mana ana.
