# He aha te mea hou mō ngā Kaiwhakahaere Whakataetae Nui

## I tukuna 2026-10-19

Koinei ngā panonitanga matua mō ngā tāngata e whakahaere ana i ngā whakataetae nui. Kei te [āwhina mō ngā kaiwhakahaere whakataetae nui](../tournament-managers/index.md) ngā taipitopito katoa.

- **Ka whakaurua ngā kaitākaro i te kotahi wā, ki te whakataetae nui.** I te pae tawhito, ka tāpiria ngā kaitākaro ki roto i ia tauwhāinga. Inaianei ko te ripa **Ngā kaitākaro** o te whakataetae nui te wāhi hei tāpiri, hei whakatika, hei whakaraupapa, hei pūrangahi i a rātou, ā, ko te kaitākaro kua whakatikahia ki reira kua whakatikahia i ia tauwhāinga e uru ana ia, i ia whārangi tūmatanui hoki. Tirohia [Ngā kaitākaro](../tournament-managers/standard-tournament/players.md).
- **Ka whakaurua ngā kaitākaro ki tētahi tauwhāinga mai i tētahi rārangi.** I te whārangi o tētahi tauwhāinga, kōwhiria he kaitākaro o te whakataetae nui mai i te rārangi taka ka pēhi i a **Tāpiri kaitākaro**. Ko ngā kaitākaro kāore anō kia uru ki te tauwhāinga anake te mea e tukuna ana e te rārangi taka. I te whakawātea ka tuku hoki i a **Bye** mō ngā wāhi toe. Tirohia [Te whakauru i ngā kaitākaro ki tētahi tauwhāinga](../tournament-managers/standard-tournament/entering-players-in-a-competition.md).
- **Mā te pātene kotahi e tāpiri he tauwhāinga.** Hei utu mō ngā pātene motuhake mō te poraka hou, te Swiss hou me te whakawātea hou, pēhia a **Tāpiri tauwhāinga**. Ka pātai te pouaka ki te ingoa, me te momo (he poraka, he whakawātea, he Swiss rānei), ā, ka pātai ki ngā mea e hiahiatia ana e taua momo: te pai rawa o te hia ia tukinga, ā, mō te whakawātea ko ngā wāhi o tōna tohu. Ka noho tuwhera kia taea ai e koe te tāpiri i te maha i te wā kotahi. Kāore e taea te whakarerekē i te momo o tētahi tauwhāinga i muri mai: pūrangahia ka tāpiri anō. Tirohia [Ngā tauwhāinga](../tournament-managers/standard-tournament/competitions.md).
- **Ngā tuhipoka a te kaiwhakahaere: whakamahia hei utu mō ngā kōrero hei kī atu ki ngā kaitākaro i ngā mea hei mōhio mā rātou.** Te whakatakotoranga, tētahi panoni ki te raupapa tākaro, te wā o te tina. Ka tuhia he tuhipoka ki te ripa **Ngā tuhipoka** o te whakataetae nui, me te taitara me te whakaahuatanga ki te Markdown, ā, ka taea e koe te arotake i mua i tō tāpiri. Ka kite ngā kaitākaro i ngā tuhipoka i te whārangi Ngā tuhipoka tūmatanui o te whakataetae nui. Ka tohua ki te whetū te tuhipoka kāore anō kia pānuitia e rātou, pērā hoki te wāhanga Ngā tuhipoka i ia whārangi o te whakataetae nui, kia tae ra ano ki te wā ka whakatuwheratia e rātou. Ka tohua anō te tuhipoka ka whakatikahia e koe mō te hunga katoa kua pānui kē. Tirohia [Ngā tuhipoka](../tournament-managers/standard-tournament/notes.md).
- **Ko ngā tuhipoka whakataetae nui o te pae tawhito ko te Taitara iti inaianei.** Ka mau tonu ngā mea i tuhia e koe ki reira hei taitara iti mō te whakataetae nui, ā, ka whakaaturia tonutia i raro i te ingoa o te whakataetae nui i ia whārangi tūmatanui. Ka tuhia ki te Markdown, ā, ka taea te 2000 tohu te roa. Mō ngā mea e tika ana kia mōhio ngā kaipānui ahakoa kei tēhea whārangi rātou. Whakamahia he tuhipoka mō ngā kōrero hou ka rerekē i a ia e haere ana.
