# Āwhina

Me pēhea te whakamahi i te pae, mā te hunga e whakamahi ana.

- [Ngā kaiwhakahaere whakataetae](tournament-managers/index.md): te whakahaere i tētahi whakataetae mai i te Kaiwhakahaere, mai i tōna waihanga ki ngā whārangi e whāia ana e ōna kaitākaro.
- [Ngā kaipānui](readers/index.md): te pānui i tētahi whakataetae i tētahi pūtirotiro, mai i te kōwhiri i te kaupapa tae me te reo ki ngā mea e hiahiatia ana e tō pūtirotiro.
- [Tō pūkete](accounts/index.md): te rēhita, te takiuru, ngā kupuhipa me te tiaki i tētahi pūkete.
- [Ngā āpiha tūnga o te ao](world-ranking-officers/index.md): ko ngā mea e hoatu ana e te rārangi whakataetae i te Kaiwhakahaere ki a koe, hāunga ngā whakataetae e whakahaere ana koe.
