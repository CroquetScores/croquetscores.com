# Kaupapahere mukunga raraunga

Te huarahi hei muku i ngā mea e puritia ana mōu e tēnei pae. He whai tikanga ahakoa te huarahi e takiuru ai koe: mā Facebook, mā Google, mā tētahi wāhitau īmēra me tētahi kupuhipa rānei.

## Te huarahi hei muku

1. Takiuru ki te pūkete e hiahia ana koe kia mukua, pēnei i ngā wā katoa.
2. Whakatuwheratia [Warewaretia te pūkete](/account/forget-account), kei te tahua pūkete hoki, me tō kōtaha.
3. Pēhia **Īmēra mai i te hono**. Kia tika ai ko koe tēnei, ka īmēra te pae i tētahi hono ki te wāhitau o tō pūkete ake. Kāore tētahi mea e mukua kia whāia ra anō taua hono.
4. Whāia te hono i te īmēra. Kotahi anō te wā e mahi ai, mō te 15 meneti, ā, ina takiuru tonu koe ki te pūkete anake. Ka whakaatu i te tikanga o te warewaretanga.
5. Pēhia **Warewaretia taku pūkete**.

Ka takiputa atu koe i ngā wāhi katoa i takiuru ai koe, ā, e kore e taea anō te takiuru ki te pūkete.

## Ngā mea ka mukua

Ko tō ingoa me tō wāhitau īmēra ka whakakapia mō ake tonu, ki ngā wāhi katoa e puritia ana e te pae, ki "Forgotten Account" me tētahi tau, me tētahi wāhitau i example.com e kore nei e a tētahi. E kore e taea e tētahi te pānui anō, tae atu ki ngā tāngata e whakahaere ana i te pae. Ka peratia anō te pūkete Google, Facebook rānei e hono ana ki tō pūkete, kia kore ai e herea te pūkete ki a koe. Ka taea ai te rēhita anō i tō wāhitau īmēra, hei pūkete hou.

## Ngā mea e puritia ana

Ka puritia te kaiwhakaatu o tō pūkete. He tau matapōkere tēnei i hangaia mō te pūkete, ā, kāore he mea e kōrero ana mō wai koe. Ka noho tonu ngā mea i whakaputaina e koe i raro i te ingoa "Forgotten Account" me taua tau: ngā whakataetae i waihangatia e koe, ngā tākohu i whakauru koe, me ngā kōrero whakamārama me ngā tākupu i tuhia e koe. He rekoata tēnei o ngā kēmu, ā, he kereme tō ngā tāngata i takaro i aua kēmu. Kāore anō e kī mai ko wai koe.

Ko tētahi kape o te pae i hangaia i mua i tō warewaretanga, pēnei i tētahi kape tautoko, kei a ia tonu ngā mea i reira i taua wā, mō te roa e puritia ana te kape.

## Ngā mea hei mōhio

- **Ehara i te mea ka mukua tētahi mea i konei ina tangohia te taupānga i ō tautuhinga Facebook.** Ka mutu noa te tiri a Facebook i ētahi atu mea ki te pae. Kia mukua ō raraunga, whāia ngā hīkoi i runga ake nei.
- **Kāore e taea te whakakore.** Koia i uiuia ai e rua ngā wā.
- **Kāore e taea te warewaretia te kaiwhakahaere kotahi anake.** Mēnā ko koe anake te kaiwhakahaere o tēnei pae, hoatu te tūranga Kaiwhakahaere ki tētahi atu i te tuatahi.
- **Mēnā kāore e taea e koe te takiuru,** whakamahia te whārangi [Kua warewaretia te kupuhipa](/account/forgot-password) hei tautuhi i tētahi kupuhipa hou, ā, ka takiuru, ka whāia ngā hīkoi i runga ake nei. Mēnā i takiuru koe mā Facebook, mā Google rānei, takiuru pērā.

Kei [te whakaaturanga matatapu](/privacy) ngā mea e puritia ana e te pae, me te take.

## Te pātai

Ko ngā mea katoa kāore i kōrerotia i konei, ko ngā pōauau rānei mō te mukunga o ō raraunga, mā ngā tāngata e whakahaere ana i te pae.
