# Testing the new Croquet Scores website

Thank you for helping. Before the new site opens to everyone, I need as many people as possible to use it the way they would on a tournament day, and to tell me where it got in their way.

The site to try is at https://beta.croquetscores.com and you can start now. If all goes well, the new site will be released publicly next week, so the sooner you try it the better.

## This is a try-out

Anything you enter may be lost or removed at any time, so please do not put in anything you need to keep.

If you already have an account on croquetscores.com, it has been copied to the try-out and you can sign in with it. What you do to the copy, such as changing its password or forgetting it, does not touch your real account, and is lost when the try-out closes.

Please use the site on a phone as well as a computer. Most people reading a score will be on a phone.

## Finding your way

The help at https://beta.croquetscores.com/help explains how to do each job. If you want to run a tournament, it has a page called "Running a standard tournament", and another called "Running a tournament played by teams" for a team event.

Use the site as you would on a real tournament day: register, create a tournament, enter players and scores, and look at what the public sees, signed in and signed out. Try the parts you would use, and the parts you would not think to.

If you get stuck, stop there and report it, rather than finding another way round. That is the most useful thing you can tell me.

## If something goes wrong

Use the **Report a bug** link in the footer of any page. It opens an email to me, with the subject already filled in.

Please say:

- what you were trying to do,
- what you expected to happen,
- what happened instead, and
- the address of the page you were on.

A screenshot helps if you can take one.

Report it as soon as it happens, not at the end of the week.

If an email from the site has not arrived after a quarter of an hour, that is worth a report too, and so is something that is awkward but works. I will weigh those at the end.

## Croquet Scores is open source

The code for the site is public, at https://github.com/CroquetScores/croquetscores.com, under the GNU Affero General Public License. You are welcome to read it, and if you can, to improve it. `CONTRIBUTING.md` in the repository says how.

Thank you for your time. Whatever you find, I am grateful for it.
